This folder is a worked example of the format. A project is one folder inside
`content/`:

- **`info.md`** — this file. `title`, `year` and `summary` are all it needs.
- **Images** — any number, AVIF, named however you like. They appear in the
  order their filenames sort, so `01-`, `02-` prefixes give you control.

Upload a new folder next to this one, commit, and it is live in about two
minutes. Then delete `content/welcome/`.
